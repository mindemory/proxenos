# Code of conduct

Proxenos is built first for people going through a hard time. Treat everyone here the way you'd want to be treated on your worst day.

- Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all. Every question is welcome.
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's personal or health information, here or anywhere it came from Proxenos.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Credit other people's work.

If someone isn't following this, or you'd rather raise something privately, use the repository's private report (the Security tab, **Report a vulnerability**, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes) and say it's a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributors that break these rules.
